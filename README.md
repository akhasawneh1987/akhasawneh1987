## Dr. Ahmad M. Khasawneh

**Associate Professor of Computer Science**
Department of Information Security, Philadelphia University, Amman, Jordan

My research focuses on routing and energy efficiency in underwater wireless sensor networks (UWSNs) and the Internet of Underwater Things, alongside network security and the Internet of Things. Most of my recent work designs depth- and pressure-based routing protocols and evaluates them in ns-3.

---

### Research interests

- Underwater wireless sensor networks and the Internet of Underwater Things
- Energy-efficient and reliable routing protocols
- Network security
- Internet of Things
