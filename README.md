Researcher and educator with more than ten years of academic experience in Jordan, the United Arab Emirates and Malaysia. My research centers on routing and energy efficiency in underwater wireless sensor networks (UWSNs) and the Internet of Underwater Things, together with network security and the Internet of Things. I design depth- and pressure-based routing protocols and evaluate them through network simulation.
